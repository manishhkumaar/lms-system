# StatCard

A single headline number for dashboards and Reports: enrolments, lead conversion, batch attendance, course completion.

**Consumer provides:** `title` (short, uppercase is applied), `value` (pre-formatted, Indian digit grouping: `1,28,400`), optional `delta` and `trend` (`up` | `down`).

- Four per row on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile.
- The delta always says what it's compared with ("vs Sept", "Last 30 days").
- Don't use a stat card for a number nobody acts on.
