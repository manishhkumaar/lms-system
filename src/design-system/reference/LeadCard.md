# LeadCard

One prospective student in the Leads Kanban: name, course of interest, source, the next interview or follow-up, and the owning Admissions Executive.

**Consumer provides:** `lead` (see `KanbanBoard`), optional `onOpen(lead)`, `dragging`.

- Opening a card shows the lead's notes and follow-up history in a side panel.
- Keep it to these fields; details live in the panel.
